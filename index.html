Questions

Common questions, answered

How does it actually work?
We connect an AI assistant to your business accounts — WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and your website. We train it on your business: what you sell, your hours, your pricing, the questions customers ask most. When a customer reaches out, the AI responds automatically with accurate answers. It's like having a staff member who never sleeps.
What kinds of businesses does this work for?
All kinds. Schools, churches, shops, salons, restaurants, clinics, builders, consultants — if you talk to customers, we can help you answer them faster. We build each assistant around your specific business, so it fits any industry.
What if the AI gives wrong information?
We monitor every conversation in real-time. If something goes wrong, we catch it and fix it — usually within minutes. The AI only answers questions we've trained it on. If something is out of scope, it hands off to you.
Will my customers know it's AI?
Some might guess, but most won't care because they get an instant answer. If a customer asks, the AI can honestly respond that it's an assistant and offer to connect them to you. What matters is they get help immediately instead of waiting.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. We're POPIA compliant — customer conversations are treated as personal information, stored securely, and never shared or sold. We can share our POPIA documentation with you on request.
How long does setup take?
About 7 days. The WhatsApp API approval takes a couple of days, then training and testing takes a few more. You're involved for a 30-minute discovery call and a 30-minute test session.
