Questions

Common questions, answered

What exactly does AutoEffortless do?
We design and deploy AI for businesses: custom apps, websites, AI email & calendar syncing, and AI booking systems. We handle the whole journey — design, build, launch, and ongoing management — so you get working AI without needing any technical skills.
Do I need any technical skills?
No. That's the point. You tell us what you need in plain language — we design, build, and run it. Want a change? Just ask, in plain language, and we make it.
What kinds of businesses does this work for?
All kinds — shops, salons, restaurants, clinics, schools, churches, builders, consultants, lodges. If your business has customers, paperwork, or bookings, there's an AI system that helps — and we build it around how you actually work.
Do you still do WhatsApp assistants?
Yes — on request, at a lower cost. It's a good starter step: an AI assistant that answers routine customer questions on WhatsApp. Many clients start there, then grow into apps, websites, and full systems.
Is my data safe?
Yes. We're POPIA compliant — your data and your customers' information are stored securely and never shared or sold. We can share our POPIA documentation on request.
How long does a project take?
Most projects go from kickoff to launch in about a week — a 30-minute discovery call, design and build, then you test it before we go live. Larger systems take longer, and we'll give you a timeline up front.
